Map key and observation rules

Start at your current workspace. Full map shows named locations; expand shared locations to choose a workspace. Drag to pan. Map keys: + / − / 0 (full map).

Stations are commits. Colored rails follow verified parent relationships; thin attachment stems identify workspaces and references. Dashed routes and hollow stops show future plans, never completed Git operations. A gap at a crossing means no connection. History is ordered by ancestry, not equal calendar intervals.

Double-ring stations mark observed forks or merges. Worktree platforms attach at HEAD; passengers are existing unarchived chats, counted once including their Agent. Finished, idle and unloaded chats remain passengers. Common ancestor means current shared history, not a recorded creation point.

Branch colors identify references. A fresh, complete chat inventory is required to confirm an unattended platform. Unattended work warns when uncommitted or unmerged changes remain. Inventory is fresh for 120 seconds. Missing observations never prove deletion; cleanup always needs review.
